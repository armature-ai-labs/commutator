# Governance

Commutator is maintained by Armature AI Labs.

- **Decisions.** The lead maintainer makes final decisions on design, releases and the safety path. Proposals start as GitHub issues so anyone can comment.
- **Safety-relevant code** (stop handling, guardrails, and later the safety filter, scheduler and motor drivers) needs a test that shows the behaviour, and maintainer review.
- **Upstream.** The routing core follows solo-router. Fixes that apply to both are offered upstream.
- **New maintainers** are invited after a sustained record of careful contributions.
